Privacy Policy – whereami

Last updated: November 21, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how whereami, developed by locario-dev, handles your information.

Location Access

The app accesses your device's location only when you press the button to check your current address. Your location is used solely to display your current address within the app.

To convert your coordinates into a human-readable address, your location is sent to Google's reverse geocoding service. This is necessary for the app to show your address.

The developer does not store or share your location or address information for any other purpose.

Information We Do Not Collect

whereami does not collect, store, or share:

  • Location history beyond the current lookup
  • Personal information
  • Analytics or usage data
  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising data

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used is the reverse geocoding service provided by Google on Android to convert your location into a readable address. No other third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used.

Permissions

The app requests location permission only when you manually trigger the request. It does not access your location in the background.

Security

All processing happens locally on your device, except for the necessary transmission of coordinates to the reverse geocoding service. No location or personal data is stored by the developer.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
